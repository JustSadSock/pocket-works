# Pocket Works Agency Guide

This file exists to prevent visual and interaction monoculture across Pocket Works applications. A new app should feel authored for its purpose, not reskinned from the previous app.

## Before designing a new app

Inspect at least the five most recently added or substantially redesigned Pocket Works apps. Write down their dominant visual material, background treatment, layout pattern, navigation model, typography character, corner/radius language, primary accent, motion style and signature interaction. Treat repeated traits as temporarily unavailable defaults.

Then define a **design fingerprint** for the new app with at least one concrete choice in each category:

1. material metaphor — e.g. ceramic, instrument panel, stitched textile, enamel signage, carved wood, ink display, raw terminal, arcade plastic;
2. composition — e.g. dense instrument face, edge-to-edge scene, radial navigation, editorial column, board/table, split workspace;
3. typography — serif/sans/mono/display relationship and density;
4. geometry — sharp, chamfered, circular, mechanical, irregular, etc.;
5. motion — one characteristic transition or physical response;
6. interaction signature — one mechanic that belongs to this app rather than the shell;
7. sound/haptics — only when they improve feedback.

At least four of these seven categories must materially differ from the recent-app set.

## Forbidden autopilot

Do not default to any of the following merely because they are easy:

- dark background + neon accent;
- glassmorphism panels;
- cream paper, paper grain, torn edges, paper cards or stationery metaphors;
- endless rounded cards floating in a uniform grid;
- oversized decorative title followed by generic cards;
- meaningless charts, counters or decorative telemetry;
- the same red horizontal rule, glow treatment or gradient used in another recent app;
- transplanting a previous app's layout and only changing colors.

These patterns are allowed only when the product concept specifically justifies them and the implementation explains that justification through function, not decoration.

## Product-first originality

Originality must improve recognition or use. Every app needs one memorable product idea: a navigation behavior, spatial metaphor, simulation principle, tactile control, information architecture, or visual system. Do not sacrifice discoverability just to be different.

The first screen must communicate what the app does and expose a meaningful action immediately. Interactive elements must react, loading and errors must be visible, empty states must lead somewhere, irreversible actions must confirm, and all visual controls must have working logic.

## Final diversity check

Before shipping, compare the finished app against the recent five again. If a screenshot with the logo removed could plausibly be mistaken for one of them, redesign the dominant surface before merging.
