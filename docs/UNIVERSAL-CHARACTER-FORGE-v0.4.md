# Universal Character Forge v0.4 — Garment Forge & Living Anatomy
Research only; v0.3 Art Forge contract remains unchanged. No production app, purchase or mass atlas.
## Garment architecture
A: drafted torso, gore-style hem, sleeve and leg tubes, cuffs, hem/neck binding, material inner/outer surfaces and thickness; measure actual body landmarks. B: one authored reusable template with stable anatomical vertex offset binding; compare same target morph, not an approved external garment. C: procedural pattern with authored fold amplitudes and weighted joints. Template is not guaranteed collision-free. Source body MakeHuman CC0; original garments, boots, seams, belt/pouch and headwear. Body faces concealed under complete garment kits; hidden anatomy kept as source. All eight components represented in four kits. Reference: https://sagy.vikingove.cz/en/construction-of-early-medieval-tunics/ ; https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2/blob/master/docs/fileformats/mhclo.md ; https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/physics/cloth/introduction.html . Optional actual Blender cloth cape settle and cross-layer triangle overlaps; approximate signed-normal audit and exact overlap counts retained, neither is proof of zero self intersections. Fold displacement is precomputed, corrective morph targets and calibrated garment simulation remain open.
## Style and identity
Low poly: broad shoulder/shorter leg proportions applied to both mesh and rest skeleton, angular panels, flat lighting. Historical miniature: independently shaped draped panels, cuffs, trims, material folds and historical color choices. Realism is untransformed source anatomy control. Six inherited body profiles + separate identity specifications: face-width/nose variation, hair/beard shape, color, posture, kits. Retain actual source face and hand geometry, no approved art yet. 
## Living animals
V4 native curved skin chains, widened muscle transitions, flat-beveled hoof mesh, shaped elephant ears and curved trunk/tusks, torso roll/pitch and rider compensation. Source-template control: two original Innerscene CC0 GLBs, actual bytes and SHA-256, reduced surface and three morphological variants each, provisional nearest-segment skinning. Source not required. References https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/standing-horse-01d8563f ; https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/african-elephant-30c02485 ; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2607390/ .
## Motion
CMU v4 computes actual source segment directions in anatomical side/up/forward basis, maps target rest segments, no IK. Static probe and four sampled phases, full cycle only on angle and lateral gap gate. Axial roll/foot orientation, root travel and contact drift not fully solved. Native v4 animal motion is a procedural candidate, not captured animal gait.
