# The Weight of a Crown — direction brief v1

Status: direction only; no score authored. Research-only. Original work.

Purpose: political power, suspicion, and a ruler's isolation; a threatening procession, not a battle, victory fanfare, or background dance. Target 2:30–3:00. No compulsory triumphant resolution.

Identity: E Phrygian modal center as candidate, flattened second as tension rather than constant gimmick; 5/4 (3+2) with a contrasting 3/4 passage, approximate quarter pulse 88–96. Low-register rhythmic identity and opposing sustained lines. Avoid D-minor-to-major architecture and eight-bar chord loops. Prefer a pedal over continuously changing triads, two independent voices and carefully placed cadences.

Reference principles (not notes or arrangements): Djawadi — withheld orchestration and narrative tension; Shore — distinctive musical worlds and deliberate thematic arrival; Valta — historical color within symphonic dramaturgy; Slavov — contrasts of character and scalable motif. See historical score direction for source links. No soundtrack audio, score or melody used.
