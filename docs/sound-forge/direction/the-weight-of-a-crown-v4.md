# The Weight of a Crown — v4: the oath and the price
Stage I, frozen before score authoring. Research-only. V2 and v3 remain immutable, unaccepted.
Purpose: a recognizable political lament that grows into a solemn procession, not a victory cue. E Phrygian with contrast in C major/A minor; modal cadences return to E. 5/4, 96 quarter BPM, 40 bars, 125 seconds plus room tail. Preserve the v3 E–F–G–B motif as the germ, not the repeated four-bar harmonies.
Reference transfer from the existing documented research: CK3 historical color and flexible texture; EU5 long melodic arcs and contrasting narrative function; KCD historical timbres inside symphonic phrasing; BG3 thematic transformation and character; Game of Thrones economical motives, withheld instrumentation, tension; LOTR harmonic identity and delayed thematic culmination. These are compositional principles, not transcriptions or claims of direct audio analysis.
Form: 4 bars sparse oath (E pedal); 8 bars theme with question/answer and cadence; 8 bars development (C and A harmonic regions, fragmented motif, independent bass); 8 bars contrast (clarinet, lyrical inversion, pulse recedes); 8 bars return (cello lead and selective violin answers, stronger bass and percussion); 4 bars price (fragment, F to E, suspended fifth). Explicit harmonic plan by bar: Em Em F Em | Em F C Am G F Am Em | C G Am F Dm C F Em | Am C G Dm F C Am Em | Em F C Am Dm G F Em | C F F Em. All pitches natural, E final. Chord tones on strong beats unless documented suspension; nonchord tones pass or resolve. Bass root/fifth rhythm 3+2, occasional inversions or anticipations, not a competing ostinato tune. Main theme spans multiple bars and transforms rhythm/contour; no continuous octave doubling. Countermelody answers in gaps or moves slowly on chord tones while lead sustains. No trumpet; horn not required, use only restrained cadential answer if feasible. Qualified cello/violin/clarinet, double bass, harp, frame drum and bass drum. No new instrument acquisition required. Check actual root and sustain coverage, never silently truncate. Keep bass audible without fixed spectral target. Use shared stereo room with differentiated sends, separate role stems. Measure active role RMS, concurrent pitches, harmony alignment, L/R correlation, mono loss, sub-120 Hz energy, peak and exact reconstruction; compare with audited v2/v3. Metrics are not artistic acceptance. Keep one final candidate, no mandatory A/B.
Soundscape slice separately: three transient families each six variations split into two contexts, field-recorded texture where qualified, 40s seamless natural ambience, 60s scene, music ducking and bounded runtime. Existing runtime API only. Preserve all old checks; no game edits, no activation.
