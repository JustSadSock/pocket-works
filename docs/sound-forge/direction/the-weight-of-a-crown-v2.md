# The Weight of a Crown — direction brief v2

Status: frozen Stage-I direction for the first Stage-II realization. Research-only, original composition. Supersedes v1 timing and source assumptions; retains the original dramatic identity.

## Dramatic purpose
A political procession about power, suspicion and the loneliness of rule. This is not a battle cue, victory anthem, court dance or generic fantasy trailer. The music should feel heavy and inevitable, with a quiet conspiracy at its centre and no compulsory triumphant resolution.

## Musical identity
- E Phrygian modal centre. The E–F semitone is a structural tension, not a decorative scale exercise.
- Quarter note = 96 BPM.
- Primary metre 5/4 grouped 3+2; one central 3/4 passage breaks the procession.
- Low double-bass ostinato, independent cello line, horn counterline and restrained high strings. Avoid constant octave doubling.
- Rhythmic language: alternating 3+2 and displaced accents, syncopated anticipations, rests and phrase-length variation. No random timing jitter.
- Harmony: E pedal with changing upper structures, modal clusters and suspensions; avoid repeated four-chord loops and major-key payoff.

- Recorded orchestral percussion: VCSL CC0, pinned revision c1ea7bcc3c7309650ab0da9d15c9cd1fbc4a4c7e; bass drum hits/crescendos and timpani hits/rolls.
- Existing qualified recorded banks: cello section, violin, horn, trumpet, clarinet, folk harp, frame drum and Renaissance organ.
- No choir, tuba or trombone is represented in this realization. No synthetic voice. No invented instrumental legato.
- Sustained string/brass phrases may use a short overlap and attack-skip as an explicitly approximate crossfade. The recorded interval coverage remains zero.

## Orchestration and space
Low strings carry the floor; cello moves independently rather than simply doubling the bass. Horns answer in the middle register; trumpet is reserved for a few structural peaks, not continuous high-register glare. Violin carries a restrained transformed motif. Clarinet owns the conspiracy section. Harp provides irregular plucked connective tissue. Frame drum is a quiet processional layer; bass drum and timpani are reserved for selected structural accents and rolls. Renaissance organ supplies a distant E/F suspension only in the Weight/Price sections.

Panning separates instrumental families. A short stone-chamber early-reflection field adds shared space while preserving attacks. Reverb must not conceal articulation or create a long wash.

## Acceptance gates
1. Every source is hash-verified, CC0 and pinned; every pitched event is inside E Phrygian and within 5.1 semitones of a measured recorded root.
2. 5/4 3+2 identity is audible, 3/4 conspiracy section is genuinely contrasting, and bass rhythm patterns are not identical across sections.
3. At least three independent melodic roles; the main motif is transformed rhythmically and harmonically, not merely repeated at higher volume.
4. Dynamic and register growth come from arrangement, not master automation alone.
5. Low-frequency foundation, mono compatibility, no clipping, and exact stem-to-master PCM reconstruction.
6. Distinguish approximate crossfades from true recorded transitions in all receipts.
7. One selected full composition for human audition. No mandatory A/B/C comparisons and no re-review of existing tracks.
