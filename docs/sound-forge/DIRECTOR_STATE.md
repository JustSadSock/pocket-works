## Mandatory Human Review Gate — owner instruction (2026-11-28)
If the director cannot establish a sufficiently reliable artistic, perceptual or other quality verdict using available evidence, explicitly withhold that verdict. Mark the exact deliverable "Human Review Required / Artistic acceptance pending", not Accepted or Rejected. Technical qualification and independent engineering findings remain separate; do not fabricate listening or substitute metrics for aesthetic judgment. This also applies to perceptual SFX realism, spatial impression, fatigue, emotional impact and other questions requiring human perception.
Every director report requiring owner input must contain a conspicuous standalone heading "Требуется твоя оценка" near the top, with exact native WAV/preview links, estimated total listening duration, the specific decision blocked, and one to three concrete listening questions. Explain what was already verified and why human input is necessary. If no owner action is needed, do not raise a review request. Unreviewed work is not implicitly approved; unrelated development may continue without waiting.
Default review budget per session: 1–3 curated recordings, total at most 5 minutes; prefer one selected candidate, use 15–30-second excerpts for localized changes and complete pieces only for evaluating form/dramaturgy. Split larger reviews across separate sessions, prioritize decisions with highest product impact, and retain all other original WAVs as optional archives. No redundant A/B/C or re-review of unchanged hashes; use matched comparisons only when they answer a meaningful unresolved question. Owner may choose a larger session explicitly. Record feedback against exact version/hash and update acceptance status only for the reviewed scope.
