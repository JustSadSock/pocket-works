# v0.3 — Morphology & Artistic Standards
Research only. No art approval, game integration or mass atlas. v1/v2 remain readable. Reuse Character Core, Transported Rings, semantic rigs, independent style/LOD, native editor-independent producer.
## Artistic rules and references
A Stylized Low Poly: clear geometric planes; planar tabard and angular hem, broad colour masses, readable hands/face preserved where they carry identity; omit micro ornaments. Prefer controlled clusters outside protected anatomy; sharp silhouette and flat lighting. Reference: https://opengameart.org/content/stylized-low-poly-character and https://visualstyles.jerrymakes.com/en/styles/low-poly/ (reference only, no copied meshes).
B Historical Miniature: proportionate body with readable garment volume; layered undergarment, flared tunic/surcoat, shoulder mantle, restrained iron/linen/leather palette, clear hem and folds. Face/hands retain source morphology. Directional key with readable shadow, neutral inspection separately. Reference: https://www.perry-miniatures.com/ ; actual 28mm historical figures, not fantasy heroic exaggeration. Do not claim historically exact wardrobe: fitted guide tights and authored geometric folds are first candidate, need sculpted clothing and equipment review.
C Anatomical Realism: original continuous CC0 body, natural adult proportion, face/digit anatomy and actual source weights, subtle material response and soft neutral lighting, fit clothes over anatomy. No fake "realism" through higher tessellation. Reference https://static.makehumancommunity.org/about/concepts/basemesh.html ; https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/creatingassets/maketarget/targets.html ; https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2/blob/master/docs/services/targetservice.md . 
All 3 candidates use same morphed source. Source meshes/target/rig from pinned MakeHuman CC0; original garments and simplified surface adapter authored here. Body mask removes intentionally clothed regions; tights are fitting guide underlayer, nearest source weight transfer and no collision guarantee. Face and hands protected before clustering; no exact topology or collision guarantee after simplification. Artist must review.
