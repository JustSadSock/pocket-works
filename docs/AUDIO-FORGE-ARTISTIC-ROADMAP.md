## Revised effort allocation
- 35% composition: memorable motifs, harmonic and rhythmic development, contrasting sections, pacing, coherent endings. Study Evening Standard and the four 8-rated tracks as positive references; do not overwrite their native masters.
- 25% orchestration and mix: instrument roles, register separation, rhythmic interplay, expressive contrast, restrained spatial balance; test on the same score.
- 15% hybrid rendering: compare procedural, recorded, and hybrid using identical score, arrangement, timing and loudness; no presumption that any backend wins.
- 10% performance intelligence and recorded intervals: preserve verified sources, prioritize techniques audible in high-rated compositions, not source count. No synthetic substitution labeled as recorded.
- 10% adaptive music and runtime: musical transitions, thematic continuity, loop seams, browser performance and safe asset delivery.
- 5% targeted source expansion: only fill specific audible gaps or essential range/articulation needs. No broad acquisition campaign until listening evidence supports it.

P0 outside allocation: resolve failing native interval CI, keep provenance and regression gates, preserve previous masters, require independent evidence. Do not weaken checks to achieve green CI. No production activation without appropriate approval.

## Next listening experiment
Produce 6–8 distinct musical tracks, target 90–150 seconds where appropriate, with recognizable motifs and development rather than a sequence of sample demos. Include lyrical chamber, historical folk, war/cinematic, and atmospheric pieces. Keep a small separate set of loudness-matched A/B/C comparisons: identical score, one experimental variable per comparison, anonymized order; report source hashes, render receipts, duration, peaks, loudness, and audio provenance. Deliver native WAV plus convenient listening copies. Explicitly distinguish scored preference from technical qualification.

- Evening Standard: immutable reference; develop a new composition in its musical direction, compare arrangement and render variants.
- March of the Vale, Iron Horizon, Last Standard, Moorland: immutable references; prioritize musical development and avoid replacing the procedural backend without demonstrated benefit.
- Market at Dusk: promising historical folk baseline; revise arrangement and test selectively added recorded expression.
- Cloister Procession: pause expansion of this particular sound palette/arrangement; diagnose composition, organ/psaltery roles and mix independently rather than assuming the instruments are defective.
- Pilgrims Crossing, Lantern Road, Afterglow Quartet: use controlled listening pairs to isolate composition, orchestration, and performance choices.
- Adaptive Journey: evaluate thematic continuity and transitions separately from individual cues.

## Artistic acceptance (proposed)
Next collection target: mean >=8/10 and at least two tracks >=9/10 in the same listener's review. Targets are not automatic pass claims. Ask for independent scores for composition, instrumentation, naturalness, and mix where possible. Technical CI and artistic review are separate gates. Maintain a non-destructive versioned audition history. No source count, benchmark or synthetic metric replaces listening.
