What this world is

КРЯЖ is an original voxel survival game.

It uses its own procedural world, block palette, creatures, UI, software voxel renderer and generated sound. No Minecraft assets, code, textures, models, logos, world data or audio are included.

The design is intentionally familiar to players of mobile voxel sandboxes: left movement, right look, a nine-slot hotbar, hold-to-mine, tap-to-place, local worlds and recipe-led survival.
