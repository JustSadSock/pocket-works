## Product Vision / Product Lock
Mobile-first global historical strategy with individual-soldier tactical battles; historically inspired Middle Ages, Renaissance and early Age of Discovery, across centuries. Procedural continuous cell-based geography, no fixed provinces. A single playable campaign connects settlement, 17-goods economy, material logistics, demography, land ownership, social institutions, dynasty, culture/religion, diplomacy, strategic and tactical land/naval war, exploration and colonization. Six existing labs remain independent and useful, not substitutes for a campaign. Multiple viable styles (agrarian, industrial, land military, merchant/transit, maritime, political) and open-ended sandbox; unequal starts and emergent history allowed; no hidden AI geography, free technology/resources or forced activity quotas. Map is primary interface. Core loop: inspect world and needs -> choose policy/investment/negotiation/operation -> observe material and political consequences -> adapt, expand or consolidate. Player should understand why events occur and have meaningful tradeoffs. Three map sizes retained; larger sizes experimental until independently qualified. Preserve offline/save behavior, v1/v2 compatibility and separate Genesis 3.4 atlas; no automatic v3 default or 0.9.0 release. Do not add major systems before current campaign is integrated and interesting.

## Creative direction
Living historical atlas, not a tactical terrain demo. Expressive geography, natural non-periodic ridges, continuous rivers/shorelines, physically grounded water/biomes/materials, inhabited landscape with cities, fields, roads, ports and borders responding to actual simulation. Spatial atlas B is primary artistic direction; orthographic A remains an alternative. This is artistic direction, not blanket renderer acceptance. Tactical scene is a living miniature historical diorama; recognizable silhouettes, equipment/formation/animation and period changes. Map readability, restrained natural palette, historical cartographic typography and warm material UI. Avoid generic web dashboard/cards, meaningless gradients, glass panels, decoration obstructing play, permanent grid, expensive photorealism. Use EU V/CK3/Victoria 3/Manor Lords/Total War as quality references, not copies. Reuse technologies from Banners and Forges only without erasing different identities; no dependency on unfinished universal technology. Geography/art target >=8/10 (director subjective assessment plus actual images, not a CI metric).
