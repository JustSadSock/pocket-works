# Natural Mountain Systems — experimental revision 3.2

No production campaign hook. 3.0/3.1 remain frozen and default newGame uses v2. Tectonic is a control only. Two new candidates: natural curved branches with varying widths, asymmetric Gaussian shoulder profiles and p-norm overlap; field synthesis blends whole 48 km real elevation regions before hydrology and classification. No subcell anchor reconstruction. DEM field synthesis receives seven quantized 250 m grids, with provenance, in experimental save parameters; this avoids network-dependent save restoration at the cost of ~700 KiB extra save payload. Rotated, offset, anisotropically transformed windows and smooth taper reduce seams; neither non-repetition nor quality is assumed before review. Reference grid scale 1 game cell = 1 km remains a research convention.

Ablation: original Tectonic vs disabled periodic fold (amplitude also changes); original Structural vs rounded cross-section or fifth-power blend; the blend is diagnostic and segment density may influence amplitude. Same seed and camera; before incision, physical bed after incision, and filled routing surface captured separately. The bed is now the visible land surface in 3.2. Lake masks and water elevations remain separate from bed; unpromoted depressions not rendered as water. Coarse physical height is retained, routingHeight is a separate downhill ordering field. 3.2 prioritizes major fine exits when building quotient routing and reports no-major-crossing vs conflicting major route; not a guarantee of exact river path agreement. A lake classified by static depth/area is not proven perennial.

DEM data are accessed from https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/ under original source conditions and attribution https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md . Original sample SHA-256 verified before conversion, provenance retained. No DEM data in production assets. Geometry-only ablations share renderer and material shader; no new art pipeline.

Tests: standard Detail 4 three control worlds, both browsers; previous neutral coordinates frozen; own-best same scoring policy; world and close; independent child process generation/restore + peak RSS for three sizes. Cross-platform hashes reported (captureStages adds beforeErosion buffer only to QA map). No 21-world campaign trial until art acceptance. This is not a released revision.
