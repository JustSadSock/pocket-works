# The Grand Campaign — six real journeys (preparation)

Product lock: ordinary Grand Campaign, natural procedural starts, no resource gifts, forced wars, quota-based trade or hidden map. Use `node apps/crown-and-marches/grand-scenario-preflight.mjs 60` to inventory starts and replay paired first decisions with the existing simulation. A blocked action or absent eligible start is evidence, not a reason to modify the start. Compare both branches from the same serialized state. This is an engine preflight, not a browser playthrough or product acceptance.

| Journey | Real initial decision | Alternative | Subsequent browser journey and consequence to inspect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agrarian | Mill | Road | Grain → milling → food availability → population/tax; check tools and logistics bottleneck. |
| Merchant republic | Lower tariff within council rules | Road | Open discovered markets, negotiate agreement, observe shipment and price/escrow; no assumed trading partner. |
| Land power | Recruit militia | Fortify | Inspect equipment, civilian population, upkeep, supply route; choose war only when reachable and politically sensible. |
| Maritime | Port or transport order | Sawmill | Compare material opportunity costs; chart a legal coastal route, dispatch expedition if eligible, observe supplies and contact. |
| Political transformation | Constitutional proposal | Tax concession | Inspect constituencies, reform progress, ruler/heir and actual dynastic opportunities; rejection is valid. |
| Survival | Fortification | Household subsidy | Compare budget/food risk, defensive geography, diplomatic access and ability to recover after scarcity. |

For later browser evidence use the normal menu/world seed/player selector, manual commands, visible explanations, pause/resume, save/reload and original screenshots. Each playthrough needs at least one observable causal chain with player choice and alternative; engine preflight cannot establish fun. Do not alter balance until failures are reproduced and traced.
