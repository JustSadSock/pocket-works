# Historical Command — communication assumptions

Arrian, Anabasis (Chinnock translation), https://www.gutenberg.org/files/46976/46976-h/46976-h.htm ; Polybius X.43–47, https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Polybius/10%2A.html ; "Can You Hear Me Now?", https://historynet.com/battlefield-communications/ . Prearranged signals are treated as simple orders only; a complex order requires a physical messenger. Couriers move toward the last known rendezvous, can search nearby, and carry dated reports. Four-tier labels and traits are gameplay abstractions, not a claim of identical documented command structures. Speech and visual signals use approximate range and simple delays; the simulation does not recreate actual ancient words or signal codes. Messenger speed, sight radius, capture distance and search time are explicit engineering assumptions. Transfers are restricted to pre-battle reorganization. No national combat modifiers.
