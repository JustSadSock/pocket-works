# Numerical laboratory — methodology correction (0.8.0)
The 150 original numbers-lab trials are archived as an exploratory protocol. At 1:1, the investigated army lost 30/30; this does not establish a causal effect of numerical superiority. Original code and evidence are deliberately unchanged. Do not pool original, v1 pilot or v2 results.

The v1 preliminary protocol established flat-map symmetry in standard and legacy WEGO, but used largely identical physical placements across seeds. Historical Command autonomous controls had no contact; 12 generated ridge cases were invalid due to water at deployments. The archived v1 reports retain these limitations and failures.

The v2 protocol pairs identical forces, objectives, intelligence, time and command model, varies initial placement with seeded mirrored offsets, then independently varies map orientation and controller assignment where applicable. Numerical ratios use initial troops including guards, not final survivors. Contact, time limit, score and initial positions are recorded; non-engagement is not discarded. Historical Command autonomous controls and symmetric authored-opening controls are separate families. Ridge tests vary only elevation, keeping water, vegetation and moisture controlled. Armor, organization, elevation and reserve-plan comparisons remain exploratory. The legacy-vs-modern label is not an a priori claim of strength. Do not infer human balance from autonomous trials.

Run: node apps/banners-of-war/control-lab-run.mjs control control-results.json (or command, material, terrain, tactics, all). Shard: node apps/banners-of-war/control-lab-run.mjs shard:0:6 results.json. The deep Universal QA suite distributes the 174 cases across six shards; smoke retains reproducibility tests. Default cases run 240 simulated seconds, three seeds, both physical orientations and both controller assignments where applicable.
