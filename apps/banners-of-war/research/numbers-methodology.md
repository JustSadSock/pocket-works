# Numerical laboratory — methodology correction (0.8.0)
The 150 original numbers-lab trials are archived as an exploratory protocol. At 1:1, the investigated army lost 30/30; this does not establish a causal effect of numerical superiority. The original code and evidence are deliberately unchanged. Do not pool old and new results.
The new control-lab.mjs pairs identical forces, objectives, intelligence, time and command model, then independently varies map orientation and controller assignment where applicable. Numerical ratios are measured using initial troops, not final survivors. Contact, time limit, score and initial balance are reported; non-engagement is a result, not silently discarded. The legacy-vs-modern controller label is not an a priori measure of strength. Historical Command and standard WEGO controls are separate. Armor, organization, terrain and a reserve-plan comparison are exploratory single-factor families, not a complete tactical balance study.
Run: node apps/banners-of-war/control-lab-run.mjs control control-results.json (or command, material, terrain, tactics, all). Default cases run 240 simulated seconds and three seeds, both physical orientations, and both controller assignments where applicable. A shorter pilot is recorded separately and must not be pooled with the full 240-second runs.
