# Endurance and intermittent contact

Philip Sabin, [The Face of Roman Battle](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-roman-studies/article/abs/face-of-roman-battle/CC48A3F1CC4230C15D76CA4EE2D8EEA7), JRS 90 (2000), 1–17; [Oxford Handbook, Rise of Rome](https://erenow.org/ww/the-oxford-handbook-of-warfare-in-the-classical-world/15.php); A. Goldsworthy, The Roman Army at War (1996). Intermittent local contact and reserve intervention are interpretative models, not a universal measured frequency. v2 separates cumulative exertion from acute strain; contact intensity depends on readiness, orientation-independent acute load, cohesion and initiative. No fixed historical outcome or scripted phase timer. Coefficients are testable gameplay assumptions. Old snapshots lacking physicsVersion retain v1 dynamics; no migration of historical results. Physical recovery at rest is capped at roughly 6.3 index points/minute, slower under threat. No instant replenishment.
