Reference board (source links, no copied copyrighted media):
- [World Archery coaching manual, Level 1](https://extranet.worldarchery.sport/documents/index.php/Coaches/Accreditation/Coaching_Levels/MANUAL_COACHING_LEVEL_1.pdf), full draw posture/shoulder alignment, anchor and straight draw wrist. Use geometry only, not modern equipment silhouette.
- [World Archery technique analysis, D'Almeida](https://www.worldarchery.sport/news/200901/nicky-hunt-investigating-technique-dalmeida-20), face contact, rear elbow alignment and expansion.
- [Valais History Museum shield](https://online.musees-valais.ch/notices/4309.html), preserved leather straps and arm padding on reverse. The protective face is on the outside of the arm; the hand closes on the inner grip. Modern reconstructions are secondary reference, not proof of exact dimensions.
- [HEMA 101 I.33 overview](https://www.hema101.com/post/i-33-a-summary-of-the-system), posture, guard and recovery. The particular shield here is enarmed, not an I.33 buckler; do not claim direct historical replication.
- [CMU subject 35](https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/search.php?subjectnumber=35), trial 35_01 walk; [BVH conversion](https://github.com/una-dinosauria/cmu-mocap), Bruce Hahne. Keep raw capture temporary; compare the derived armed model motion, not just angles. No production asset import.
- [Woodland Trust Symonds Yat West](https://www.woodlandtrust.org.uk/visiting-woods/woods/symonds-yat-west/), river as an uninterrupted shape, woodland masses and open meadows; not a literal reproduction of a different physical map. [Forest floor scan](https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_floor) and [stony dirt path scan](https://polyhaven.com/a/stony_dirt_path), CC0. Keep restored contour water topology, real terrain heights and projection.

Wrist continuity gate uses absolute quaternion dot and loop closure; conservative 12 degree neighboring-sample and 25 degree reference deviation limits, plus a 35 degree forearm/palm angle check. These are workshop constraints for this specific motion, not medical universal human ROM limits. Shield board center and normal are checked relative to actual arm and torso. BVH mesh intersections and evaluated joint/mesh persistence remain mandatory. Bow static gate is strict: off-axis <= 2.5 cm, arrow angle <= 3 degrees, string length error <= 1 mm, actual anchor contact and rear elbow. Any violation retains evidence and rejects candidate.
