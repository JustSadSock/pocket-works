## Sandspiel

Repository: `MaxBittker/sandspiel`
License: MIT License
Copyright: Copyright (c) 2018 Max Bittker

Sandspiel was used as a design and architecture reference for the falling-sand genre. ВЕЩЕСТВО does not embed Sandspiel, does not include its interface, fonts, artwork, deployment code or community features, and does not copy its original application shell.

Concepts studied and independently reimplemented:

- discrete cellular simulation on a dense grid;
- local neighbor-based material rules;
- separation of simulation update and pixel rendering;
- browser-first high-frequency interaction.

Material changes and additions in ВЕЩЕСТВО:

- independent TypeScript engine and data format;
- temperature, heat capacity, conductivity and phase transitions;
- pressure field, structural damage and blast waves;
- electrical charge, conductors, sparks and electrolysis;
- visual no-code material synthesis and rule editor;
- rule-loop safety validation and active-particle budget;
- analytical layers, sensors, graphs, tasks and automatic goal checks;
- mobile pinch/pan camera, scale-invariant brush radius, undo/redo and rewind;
- local IndexedDB persistence, migrations and offline PWA packaging.
