# Changelog

- First Pocket Works release.
- Added 56 built-in and service materials.
- Added movement, phase transitions, heat, pressure, combustion, chemistry, electricity, destruction and biological systems.
- Added 21 direct-manipulation tools and camera gestures.
- Added visual material synthesis with reaction validation.
- Added 15 prepared experiments and 15 automatically checked tasks.
- Added rewind, undo/redo, sensors, analytics, autosave, import/export and full offline PWA support.
