Fog of Conquest

Turn-based strategy. Capture or destroy all enemy bases.

Units:
• Swordsman — move 2, attack 2, defense 1, range 1, 5 HP, 3 gold.
• Archer — move 2, attack 3, defense 0, range 2, 4 HP, 4 gold.
• Heavy — move 1, attack 3, defense 2, range 1, 6 HP, 5 gold.
• Rider — move 3, attack 3, defense 1, range 1, 5 HP, 7 gold.
• Mage — move 2, attack 0, defense 0, range 1, 4 HP, 7 gold; heals adjacent allies.
• Bog (beta) has unique abilities.

Buildings:
• Base trains basic troops.
• Barracks, stable and mage tower unlock new units.
• Mills bring gold each turn.
• Fort grants defense bonus.

Terrain:
Forest and hills give defense, water slows, mountains impassable.

Gameplay:
• Capture buildings to get gold and recruit units.
• Income from resources each turn.
• Move units and attack, then press End turn.
• Fog of war hides unexplored areas.
Win by destroying all enemy bases and troops.

Controls:
• Left click to select units and cells.
• Right click to cancel selection.
• ℹ️ shows the legend; "Show map" is only available in beta.
